Caroline Escoubas, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2014-2018

Caroline Escoubas joined the MAIR Lab in 2014 as a visiting M.D./Ph.D. student from the University of Nice, France, and completed Ph.D. research in the lab through 2018.

Caroline's first-author work in the lab includes AMPK modulates associative learning via neuronal mitochondrial fusion in C. elegans (bioRxiv 2020) and Deregulation of CRTCs in Aging and Age-Related Disease Risk (Trends in Genetics 2017).
