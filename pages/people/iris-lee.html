Chia-Lin (Iris) Lee

In the MAIR Lab 2016

Chia-Lin (Iris) Lee joined the MAIR Lab in 2016 for a research placement as a visiting master's student from the Institute of Biomedical Engineering at National Cheng Kung University, Taiwan, where Iris studied aging in C. elegans.

Iris is an author on the lab's study showing that neuronal TORC1 modulates longevity via AMPK and cell nonautonomous regulation of mitochondrial dynamics (eLife 2019).
