Postdoctoral Fellow

Jamie Kraft, PhD

In the MAIR Lab since 2025 · Swedish Research Council Fellow

Jamie Kraft is a postdoctoral fellow interested in how periods of fasting can impact metabolic health and aging, with particular interest in the role of the microbiome. She completed her PhD in the Bäckhed Lab at the University of Gothenburg, where she studied gut microbe-host interactions in metabolic disease, and joined the MAIR Lab in 2025 as a Swedish Research Council International Postdoctoral Fellow. Jamie is an author on the lab’s study showing that peroxisomes orchestrate metabolic flexibility and longevity through an interorganelle cascade (Nature Aging 2026).
