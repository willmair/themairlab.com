Kristopher Burkewitz, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2012-2018

Kristopher Burkewitz was a postdoctoral fellow in the MAIR Lab from 2012 to 2018, supported by NIH/NIA F32 and K99/R00 awards.

Kristopher's first-author work in the lab includes Atf-6 Regulates Lifespan through ER-Mitochondrial Calcium Homeostasis (Cell Reports 2020), Neuronal CRTC-1 governs systemic mitochondrial metabolism and lifespan via a catecholamine signal (Cell 2015) and AMPK as a Pro-longevity Target (Experientia Supplementum 2016).
