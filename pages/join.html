How we work

What it’s like here

A lab is its people. These are the things we care about.

Rigor first

We would rather be right than first. Clear questions, careful controls and honest data are the basis of everything we do.

Big questions, shared tools

We aim at fundamental problems in aging and build tools and resources that the whole community can use.

Mentorship you can see

The lab runs on a written mentoring compact and lab manual, structured one-on-ones, an annual anonymous lab-climate survey that we discuss together, and a yearly “State of the Lab.” 22 trainees have gone on to faculty, industry and postdoctoral positions.

Everyone belongs

Science is better when it includes people from every background. We work to make the lab a place where everyone can do their best work.
