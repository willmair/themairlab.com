Postdoctoral Fellow

Christopher Morrow, PhD

In the MAIR Lab since 2021 · NIA F32 and K99/R00 awardee

Christopher Morrow joined the MAIR Lab as a postdoctoral fellow in 2021. Chris holds an NIH/NIA K99/R00 award for the project Peeling Back the Layers of Mitochondrial Aging.

Chris is first author of the lab's mito-NAD(P)H age clocks: a label-free, non-destructive imaging method in which endogenous mitochondrial NAD(P)H fluorescence predicts biological age and remaining lifespan at subcellular resolution (Communications Biology 2024).
