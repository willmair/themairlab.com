Website of the Metabolism and Aging Integrative Research (MAIR) Lab, PI William (Will) Mair, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. Live at https://themairlab.com.

## How the site works
- Plain static site. Page sources: `pages/*.html` (front-matter comment at top). Shared header/footer/nav: `scripts/build.py`. **Run `python3 scripts/build.py` after any change** — root `*.html` files are generated.
- Data: `assets/js/people.js` (current + alumni with `group`/`years`/`now`), `assets/js/news.js` (news/media; podcasts can have `links: {spotify, apple, youtube}`; auto-sorted newest first), `assets/js/pubs.js` (generated by `python3 scripts/update_pubs.py`, CV-gated; non-CV PubMed hits go to `scripts/pending_pubs.json`).
- Profile pages: `pages/people/<slug>.html` → built to `people/<slug>.html`, for current members and for alumni who published with the lab (alumni with no lab paper or preprint get no page). Bios use CV facts only. Cards and alumni names on the People page link there via `slug` in people.js. Each page's publications come from `assets/js/people_pubs.js`, generated by `python3 scripts/update_people_pubs.py` (run after update_pubs.py): lab papers from pubs.js matched on full first name in PubMed, CV items not on PubMed (CV_EXTRA), each person's ORCID works (ORCID dict, verified iDs only; shown as "Other publications"), and for current members also their vetted outside papers (CONFIRMED PMIDs; new name hits go to `scripts/pending_people_pubs.json`). A new page needs an entry in PEOPLE in that script.
- Portraits: `assets/img/people/`, 4:5, eyes aligned, original backgrounds kept; how to make one in `scripts/portraits/README.md`.
- Styles: `assets/css/style.css`; scripts: `assets/js/main.js` (hero wave, contact form, lab-age clock, renderers).
- Brand working files: `brand/` (gitignored; logo build scripts, PowerPoint template builder, fonts). Published brand kit: `OneDrive/01 Lab/Brand & Website/MAIR Lab Brand/`.
- Local preview: `python3 -m http.server 8766` in this folder → http://localhost:8766
