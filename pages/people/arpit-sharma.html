Arpit Sharma, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2018-2025

Arpit Sharma was a postdoctoral fellow in the MAIR Lab from 2018 to 2025, supported by an NIH/NIA K99/R00 Pathway to Independence award.

Arpit's first-author work in the lab includes Peroxisomes orchestrate metabolic flexibility and longevity via an interorganelle cascade (Nature Aging 2026) and Causal roles of mitochondrial dynamics in longevity and healthy aging (EMBO Reports 2019).
