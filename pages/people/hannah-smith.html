Hannah Smith, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2018-2024

Hannah Smith completed a Ph.D. in the MAIR Lab from 2018 to 2024, in the Biological Sciences in Public Health program at Harvard, supported by an NIH/NIA F31 fellowship.

Hannah's first-author work in the lab includes Neuronal mTORC1 inhibition promotes longevity without suppressing anabolic growth and reproduction in C. elegans (PLoS Genetics 2023) and Metabolic Communication and Healthy Aging: Where Should We Focus Our Energy? (Developmental Cell 2020).
