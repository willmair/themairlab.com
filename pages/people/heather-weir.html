Heather Weir, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2014-2017

Heather Weir was a postdoctoral fellow in the MAIR Lab from 2014 to 2017.

Heather's first-author work in the lab includes Dietary Restriction and AMPK Increase Lifespan via Mitochondrial Network and Peroxisome Remodeling (Cell Metabolism 2017) and SnapShot: Neuronal Regulation of Aging (Cell 2016).
