Yue Zhang, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2012-2018

Yue Zhang completed a Ph.D. in the MAIR Lab from 2012 to 2018, in the Biological Sciences in Public Health program at Harvard.

Yue's first-author work in the lab includes Neuronal TORC1 modulates longevity via AMPK and cell nonautonomous regulation of mitochondrial dynamics in C. elegans (eLife 2019) and Dietary restriction in C. elegans (Springer book chapter, 2016).
