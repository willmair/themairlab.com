Support us

Help us add healthy years to life.

Philanthropy lets us pursue bold, early-stage ideas that are rarely funded by traditional grants, and keeps discovery moving when federal support is uncertain.

What your support makes possible

Where gifts go

Gifts of any size can support the lab’s research, our trainees, or the Harvard Chan Healthy Aging Initiative.

New ideas

Seed funding for high-risk, high-reward projects on metabolic flexibility and aging, so promising early results can grow into larger studies.

The next generation

Support for the postdoctoral fellows, students and research staff who do the work, and who go on to lead labs, companies and programs of their own.

Continuity

A buffer that keeps experiments, people and long-term studies going through gaps and uncertainty in federal funding.

Healthy aging for everyone

Work through the Healthy Aging Initiative that connects the biology of aging to public health, and to equity in who gets to age well.
