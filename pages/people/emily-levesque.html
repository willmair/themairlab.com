Emily Levesque

In the MAIR Lab since 2026

Emily joined the MAIR Lab in 2026 as a staff research assistant. She is interested in how environmental inputs such as food, nutrients and fasting shape metabolic flux and homeostasis, and in the genetic mechanisms behind these changes in the aging animal. She is currently studying how AMPK agonists (metformin) influence growth rate and lifespan in animals with altered cellular energy metabolism.
