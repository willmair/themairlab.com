SKI LODGE

Single-copy knock-in loci for defined gene expression in C. elegans.

About Overview Step by step guide Strains

About

We have generated a single-copy knock-in loci for defined gene expression (SKI LODGE) system to insert any DNA by CRISPR/Cas9 at defined safe harbors in the C. elegans genome. Utilizing a single crRNA guide, which also acts as a Co-CRISPR enrichment marker, any DNA sequence can be introduced as a single copy, regulated by different tissue-specific promoters. The SKI LODGE system provides a fast, economical and effective approach for generating single-copy ectopic transgenes in C. elegans.

Silva-García CG, Lanjuin A, Heintz C, Dutta S, Clark NM, Mair WB. Single-copy knock-in loci for defined gene expression in Caenorhabditis elegans. G3 (Bethesda) 2019; 9(7): 2195-2198. doi:10.1534/g3.119.400314

Overview

The SKI LODGE cassettes were introduced into intergenic regions that are known to give stable expression.

The SKI LODGE system allows insertion of single-copy transgenes in C. elegans into safe harbor loci using CRISPR/Cas9 editing. Utilizing the dpy-10 crRNA guide both for knock-in and Co-CRISPR edits, the gene of interest can be introduced as a single copy, regulated by different tissue-specific promoters.

Summarized protocol

  1. Select the SKI LODGE strain to express the gene of interest.
  2. Design primers with homologous recombination arms (~35 bp) for the SKI LODGE cassette (review sequences in the step by step guide and the strains below).
  3. Assemble the CRISPR/Cas9 complex in vitro.
  4. Inject.
  5. 3-4 days after injection, screen for dumpy/roller worms.
  6. Single out worms and look for fluorescent expression or genotype them.
  7. Outcross.
