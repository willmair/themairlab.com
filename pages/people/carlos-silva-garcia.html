Carlos Silva-García, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2015-2023

Carlos Silva-García was a postdoctoral fellow in the MAIR Lab from 2015 to 2023, supported by a Yerby Fellowship and an NIH/NIA K99/R00 Pathway to Independence award.

Carlos's first-author work in the lab includes The CRTC-1 transcriptional domain is required for COMPASS complex-mediated longevity in C. elegans (Nature Aging 2023), Single-Copy Knock-In Loci for Defined Gene Expression in Caenorhabditis elegans (G3 2019) and Confirming the pro-longevity effects of H3K4me3-deficient set-2 mutants in extending lifespan in C. elegans (bioRxiv 2022).
