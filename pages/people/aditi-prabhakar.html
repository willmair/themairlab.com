Postdoctoral Fellow

Aditi Prabhakar, PhD

In the MAIR Lab since 2021 · Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation Postdoctoral Fellow

Aditi studies how cells integrate information on their nutritional state to make decisions about metabolism, and how these processes change with age. Her work focuses on mTORC1, a central growth regulator, and a major target in aging research. She found an interaction between two of its upstream regulators, Rheb and GATOR1 (bioRxiv 2025), and uses genetics and biochemistry in worms and human cells to understand how cells coordinate nutrient and growth factor signals. Her broader goal is to understand how cellular signaling becomes dysregulated with age and identify more precise ways to support healthy aging.
