Miriam Valera-Alberni, PhD

In the MAIR Lab 2021-2025

Miriam Valera-Alberni was a postdoctoral fellow in the MAIR Lab from 2021 to 2025.

Miriam's first-author work in the lab includes Novel imaging tools to study mitochondrial morphology in Caenorhabditis elegans (Life Science Alliance 2024), Fast fragmenting mitochondria by TORC2 (Nature Cell Biology 2023) and Nutrient-Responsive Formation of Mitochondrial-Derived Structures in Caenorhabditis elegans (bioRxiv 2025).
